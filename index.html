🎓 The Three Habits Behind Every Setting

  • Audience first. Almost every leak is a default set to "Public" or "Everyone". If a post doesn't need the world, it shouldn't have the world.
  • Discoverability is a door. Being findable by phone number or email connects your accounts to each other — and to anyone holding a leaked contact list.
  • Review after updates. Apps reshuffle privacy menus and occasionally reset choices. A five-minute quarterly re-check keeps today's work from quietly expiring.
